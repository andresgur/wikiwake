# Wiki Wake – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Wiki Wake is an Android alarm app that reads a random Wikipedia article aloud.

## Data we collect
None. Wiki Wake has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store on our servers, or share personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Your alarms and settings (language, topics, voice, speed, pitch, reading time) are stored locally on your phone only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Network requests
To fetch articles, the app connects directly to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) APIs. Wikipedia receives your IP address, the language you selected and, if you chose topics, your search terms. See the [Wikimedia Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

## Permissions
- Notifications: show the alarm and a Stop button.
- Exact alarms: ring at the time you set.
- Foreground service / wake lock: keep reading while the screen is off.
- Internet: download articles.

## Text-to-speech
Speech is generated by the voice engine installed on your phone. Some "online" voices (e.g. Google) may send text to their provider; see that provider's policy.

## Content
Article text comes from Wikipedia and is available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
